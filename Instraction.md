## 🧠 NEURAL RESET PROTOCOLS

### 🧊 SHAME SPIRAL BREAKER

**If you feel frozen, paralyzed, or "I've already wasted the day":**

1. **Physical Anti-Collapse (2 minutes):**
   - Stand up
   - Lengthen spine by 5%
   - Open chest slightly
   - Look up at ceiling for 10 seconds
   - Say out loud: *"I am allowed to be a work in progress"*

2. **Box Breathing (2 minutes):**
   - Inhale: 4 seconds
   - Hold: 4 seconds
   - Exhale: 4 seconds
   - Hold: 4 seconds
   - Repeat 6 times

3. **Oxytocin Release:**
   - Place both hands firmly on your chest
   - Apply gentle pressure
   - Hold for 30 seconds
   - This deactivates amygdala fear response

4. **Return to [PROTOCOL B: FROZEN](#protocol-b-frozen)**

---

### ⚡ DOPAMINE CIRCUIT BREAKER

**If you keep reaching for phone/distractions:**

1. **20-Foot Rule (Immediate):**
   - Take phone to different room
   - Close door behind you
   - Don't bring it back for 25 minutes

2. **Tactile Reset (5 minutes):**
   - Wipe down your desk
   - Organize 3 items
   - Physical completion = dopamine hit

3. **Environmental Anchor:**
   - Change location (different room/desk)
   - Or change position (stand instead of sit)
   - Brain associates location with behavior

4. **Return to [PROTOCOL A: SCROLLING](#protocol-a-scrolling)**

---

### 🌊 CORTISOL FLUSH

**If you feel anxious, overwhelmed, heart racing:**

1. **Cold Exposure (30 seconds):**
   - Splash ice-cold water on face
   - Or hold ice cube in hand
   - Activates mammalian dive reflex
   - Heart rate drops, mind clears

2. **Bilateral Movement (10 minutes):**
   - Walk outside (no phone)
   - Or march in place
   - Helps brain process emotional residue

3. **Worry Dump:**
   - Write all anxious thoughts on paper
   - Physically put paper in drawer
   - "Offload the RAM" of working memory

4. **Return to [PROTOCOL C: TIRED](#protocol-c-tired)**

## 💪 FLOW STATE PREPARATION

### PREREQUISITES FOR FLOW

**Flow requires specific conditions:**

1. **Balanced dopamine baseline** (not depleted by scrolling)
2. **Clear single goal** (not scattered across 5 tasks)
3. **Skill/challenge match** (not too easy, not overwhelming)
4. **Uninterrupted time** (minimum 90 minutes)
5. **Environmental control** (no phone, no distractions)

**Hyperfocus vs Flow:**

| Hyperfocus (ADHD) | Flow State (Mastery) |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Involuntary | Voluntary |
| On low-value tasks | On meaningful work |
| Sticky, hard to stop | Natural completion |
| Followed by regret | Followed by satisfaction |
| Dopamine-seeking | Dopamine-optimized |

**You want Flow, not Hyperfocus.**
